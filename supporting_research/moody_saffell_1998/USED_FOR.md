# Moody & Saffell (1998), "Reinforcement Learning for Trading", NIPS 11

File: reinforcement_learning_for_trading.pdf (downloaded).

Note: this is the earlier version of the 2001 IEEE paper cited in the proposal. The 2001 paper itself was not available to read, so check its numbers before citing them.

Used for:
- Checking how much run-to-run spread the original RL-trading work reported: 30 runs for their RL trader (RRL), 10 for their Q-learning trader, shown as box plots only, with no numeric std in the text.
- Their Sharpe numbers on monthly S&P 500, 1970-1994: buy-and-hold 0.34, Qtrader voting 0.63, RRL voting 0.83.
